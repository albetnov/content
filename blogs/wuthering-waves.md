## Combat Mechanics

The game has plenty of mechanics, but for now, let's discuss the Combat Mechanics. Wuthering Waves features Attack & Parry with Dodge. This mechanic is quite common in Souls-like games. But it's also here, in Wuthering Waves! Let's discuss them.

### Concerto Energy

This is an attack that occurs when a character enters/leaves battle (when you switch characters). Depending on the character doing so is often beneficial. You can have effects such as damage buff, continuous damage, buffs for the new character (the character you switch into), or negative effects/nerfs for the enemy.

:custom-image{src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dltzvdmck/image/upload/v1723902045/blogs/jmovz3solxlmxrkz8rd6.png" alt="Havoc Rover outro"}

> Rover continuous damage when leaving (outro skill)

### Dodge

Dodge is like its name, to dodge the attack from the enemy. Prevent any damage to your character if you manage to dodge it in split seconds. There's also Dodge Counter (is that what it's called?) you can use this the moment you just finished dodging, just strike back. Your character would deal a ton of damage and it will be True Damage as well (which means the enemy's damage reduction/defenses).

:custom-image{src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dltzvdmck/image/upload/v1723901806/blogs/aoin7hjyhouiykfm5a25.png" alt="Dodge"}

### Counterattack

Counterattack is yet another mechanic that I highly suggest that you master it. By performing counter-attack you can stun the enemy for a few seconds. Cancelling their attacks and more. The game also shows a light yellow circle indicating the time for you to attack the enemy

:custom-image{src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dltzvdmck/image/upload/v1723901807/blogs/yiliofsnr6vdhgmlop2i.png" alt="Counterattack"}

> TIP: Consider wait a while the moment the yellow circle appear and your rate of successfully hitting it will be higher, alternatively once the yellow circle appear make sure to have concerto energy stacked and switch into character that have attack type intro skill to have higher success rate of countering attack. 


That should be all, the rest are just skills and ultimate skill also some passive that depend on the character you are playing.

> TIP: For the above reasons, I would suggest you build a ranged attack character (preferably Chixia). Ranged Characters are often easier to perform mechanics such as Dodge Counter and Counter Attack where both are powerful mechanics and can be changing factors when fighting tough enemies.

Main Quests are as you can expect, the quests that are related to the story. Sometimes, to play these quests you need to level up your union level first.

Companion Quest. I'll consider this as my favorite quest. In this quest, we will listen to the story of a specific character. Unfortunately, this quest is for limited-time characters only. ### Characters

At this time of writing, Wuthering Waves has 21 characters. Most of the characters are DPS-type. The gap between 4 to 5-star characters is quite huge, moreover for upgrades and their maximum stats. With some trade-off, you might find 4-star characters worth using. Of 21 of these characters, 12 are 5-star and 9 left are 4-star characters.

Also of those 21 characters, only 2 of them have strong enough healing support (Baizhi 4-star and Verina 5-star). Most of them are sword-type DPS and some are ranged. This makes it more annoying because you would need support and you already have a lot of DPS characters... So I honestly think the game needs to rebalance. This matters because the game features Tower of Adversity, a challenge with limited energy for each character.

> Tip: If you are new to the game, you might have a chance to be at an event where you can choose a 5-star character to draw. In this case, do pick Verina. I don't even have her at this point and could only relay to Baizhi

### Upgrades

Just like any other free-to-play online RPG, upgrading is sucks and time-consuming. The same case, for this game. Let's break it down.

#### Resonator Upgrade

:custom-image{src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dltzvdmck/image/upload/v1723908760/blogs/qz8njltbgqqhgatavyn6.png" alt="Jinhsi Upgrade"}

In Resonator Upgrading, the important point is level up and ascending. Depending on your character stars and type also the level, the upgrade materials required vary. Usually, 5-star characters are often twice as expensive when compared to 4-star.

Also depending on your union level, you might need fewer grinds as a higher union level grants you a higher rate of rewards.

> TIP: Priotioze your Union Level and only upgrade the character that you put in the party frequently. You can upgrade the rests once you reach high enough Union Level, this can save the amount you need for grinding. Or if you don't have anything to do again, you can consider upgrading the other character.

#### Weapon Upgrade

:custom-image{src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dltzvdmck/image/upload/v1723909035/blogs/ocmashm9xftvr6fyqshm.png" alt="Jinhsi holding sword"}

Similar to Resonator Upgrade, the sword also has ascension and level-up. With additional rank, where you can merge 2 weapons into one. But rank won't level up your weapon star lol.

> I would suggest you open YouTube and search for 4 or 5-star weapon locations and survey them. Until you run out of options you can use 3-star weapons. Higher star weapons have better effects which contributes to overall stats.

#### Echo Upgrades

:custom-image{src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dltzvdmck/image/upload/v1723909319/blogs/wy1kobwmvdgddzulqirg.png" alt="Echo Upgrade"}

Now for the echo part, congratulations, you have 5 separate things to upgrade. In Echo Upgres, there are just levels up and turning. No more ascension! Level-up is straightforward, but tuning is not. In tuning you would gacha again! Oh let's not forget that to unlock tune slots, you have to level up the echo first. So if you have bad luck and got a bad tune, then simply do it all over again with the new echo replacement.

:custom-image{src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dltzvdmck/image/upload/v1723909579/blogs/ja9hysew7qijb8vtg4ow.png" alt="Data Bank"}

I would suggest you reach level 18 in Data Bank at minimum (level 15 at the bare minimum) before upgrading echoes. Once you reach them, it will be easy for you to farm 5-star echo. Reaching level 21 will even allow you to have a 100% enhanced rate of 5 stars.

#### Skills Upgrades

:custom-image{src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dltzvdmck/image/upload/v1723909364/blogs/xgihpuy7p8n9wvjwbmce.png" alt="Skill Upgrades"}

Wuthering Waves characters have a skill, ultimate skill, and basic attack. But for some reason in skill upgrades, they decided to split it too many. Resulting in you having to grind to your tears just to upgrade them all. Oh, they also cost a lot of credits! So fight on! Go there and waste your time for a game!

#### Resonance Chain

:custom-image{src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dltzvdmck/image/upload/v1723909356/blogs/uglb6qexkm6i53oioq0u.png" alt="Resonance Chain"}

Not only do other upgrades cost a lot of time and credits, but an online RPG is not complete without this kind of upgrade. Resonance Chain is character merging. If you draw a character you already have it will be converted to one of this chain. To fulfill my Jinhsi I just had to draw her again 6 more times! Great!

> Resonance Chain is one of the most powerful upgrades, usually getting 4-star character resonance is not so hard. If you got them consider comparing them to your other 5-star. It might be more worth it to focus on upgrading.


#### Gacha System

For 10 pulls you will need 1600 asterites. On average you might be able to get 50-100 asterites daily by doing Guidebook and Daily Missions. With the event, you could have Radiant Tide (for limited banner pull) and Lustrous Tide (for normal banner pull).

As of the time of writing this blog, I am only unable to get Yinlin's character as part of the Limited Time Banner. Also Xiangli Yao is free. I played Wuthering Waves as F2P this far, and have never pulled 5-star weapons. So I guess the Gacha system is pretty fair.
